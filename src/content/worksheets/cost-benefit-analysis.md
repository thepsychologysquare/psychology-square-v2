## What Is Cost-Benefit Analysis?

Cost-benefit analysis is a practical decision-making technique that involves considering the potential advantages and disadvantages associated with something you are currently weighing. In psychological and therapeutic contexts, this type of structured exercise can help individuals examine their thinking more deliberately and recognize factors that may otherwise be overlooked.

The purpose is not to tell you which decision to make. Instead, the worksheet provides a framework for **self-reflection and clearer decision-making**. It can be particularly useful when a choice feels complicated, emotionally difficult, or associated with competing considerations.
