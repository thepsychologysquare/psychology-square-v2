# Discounting the Positives Worksheet

The **Discounting the Positives Worksheet** from The Psychology Square is a practical psychology resource designed to support more balanced thinking and self-reflection. It can be particularly useful when someone finds it difficult to recognize positive aspects of their life or tends to view a situation primarily through its negative aspects.

When we are experiencing stress, disappointment, low mood, or ongoing problems, it can become easy to develop an overly negative view of ourselves or our circumstances. Positive experiences may receive less attention, while difficulties can feel more prominent. This can influence how we interpret relationships, personal circumstances, achievements, and everyday experiences.

This worksheet provides a structured opportunity to step back from that perspective and examine a situation more deliberately. It encourages **objectivity, balanced reflection, and awareness of both positive and negative aspects** rather than automatically allowing one side to dominate the way a situation is perceived.

## What Is Discounting the Positives?

**Discounting the positives** is a cognitive pattern in which positive experiences, achievements, circumstances, or qualities may be overlooked, minimized, or given less importance than negative information.

This pattern can contribute to an increasingly negative interpretation of a person's life or a particular situation. Developing awareness of how attention is distributed between positive and negative information can therefore be a useful part of psychological self-reflection.

The goal of this worksheet is not to force positive thinking or deny genuine problems. Instead, it provides a way to consider a situation with greater **balance and objectivity**.

## Benefits of Using a Structured Reflection Worksheet

Putting thoughts into a structured format can make patterns in thinking easier to notice. Instead of relying solely on an immediate emotional impression, written reflection can create some psychological distance from the situation being considered.
