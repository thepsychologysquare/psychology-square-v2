Self-regulation is not about suppressing emotions; it is about managing how you respond to them. It sits in the space between stimulus and response.

* The Pause: When a strong emotion hits, your brain’s amygdala reacts instantly. Taking a brief pause creates space for the logical prefrontal cortex to engage.
* Reframing: Instead of asking *"Why is this happening to me?"*, shift your perspective to *"What is this situation teaching me?"* or *"What is the most constructive response here?"*
* Action Item: Use the 6-Second Rule. When triggered, wait 6 seconds before responding verbally or via email. This allows the emotional surge to subside enough for reason to catch up.
