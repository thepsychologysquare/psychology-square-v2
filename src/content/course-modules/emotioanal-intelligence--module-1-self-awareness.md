Self-awareness is the bedrock of EQ. It is the ability to recognize your emotions in real time and understand their impact on your thoughts and behavior.

* Emotional Literacy: Most people default to simple labels like "fine," "angry," or "stressed." High EQ requires pinpointing specific emotions—such as distinguishing between feeling *overwhelmed* versus feeling *unsupported*.
* Identifying Triggers: Pay attention to what reliably causes an emotional spike (e.g., being interrupted, feeling dismissed).
* Action Item: Practice the 3x3 Check-In. Three times a day, pause for 30 seconds and ask yourself: *What am I feeling right now, and where do I feel it in my body?*
